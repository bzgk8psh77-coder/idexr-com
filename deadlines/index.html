Deposit deadlines

Typical statutory windows for returning a residential security deposit in the United States. Confirm the current statute — legislatures move. This table is a starting clock, not a holding.

Ready to write? Open the deposit packet.

StateDaysStatuteIf they keep it
Alabama60Generally 60 days after termination.Ala. Code § 35-9A-201Wrongful withholding can support actual damages plus possible statutory penalties under the UTA.
Alaska1414 days if no deductions; 30 days if itemized deductions.Alaska Stat. § 34.03.070Up to twice the amount wrongfully withheld.
Arizona1414 business days after termination and demand.Ariz. Rev. Stat. § 33-1321Twice the amount wrongfully withheld.
Arkansas6060 days after termination.Ark. Code § 18-16-305Double the deposit plus costs in some cases.
California2121 days after the tenant vacates.Cal. Civ. Code § 1950.5Up to twice the deposit in addition to actual damages for bad-faith retention.
Colorado30One month, unless the lease says less (not more than 60 days by agreement in some cases).Colo. Rev. Stat. § 38-12-103Treble damages for willful and wrongful withholding.
Connecticut3030 days, or 15 days after receiving a forwarding address if later.Conn. Gen. Stat. § 47a-21Double damages plus attorney’s fees in specified cases.
Delaware2020 days after termination.25 Del. C. § 5514Double the amount wrongfully withheld.
District of Columbia4545 days after the tenancy ends.D.C. Code § 42-3502.17Treble damages possible for bad-faith withholding.
Florida1515 days if no claim; 30 days to give written notice of a claim against the deposit.Fla. Stat. § 83.49Landlord may forfeit the right to the deposit if notice rules are missed.
Georgia3030 days after termination, or as the lease provides if not longer.O.C.G.A. § 44-7-35Possible forfeiture of the right to withhold plus costs.
Hawaii1414 days after termination.Haw. Rev. Stat. § 521-44Amount wrongfully withheld plus possible damages and fees.
Idaho2121 days after vacancy, or 30 days if specified in writing.Idaho Code § 6-321Treble damages for bad-faith refusal.
Illinois3030 days for properties of 5+ units (45 days if itemized deductions); smaller buildings may differ.765 ILCS 710 / 715Double the deposit plus attorney’s fees in many cases.
Indiana4545 days after the tenant delivers possession.Ind. Code § 32-31-3-12Court may award the deposit plus damages and costs.
Iowa3030 days after termination and demand.Iowa Code § 562A.12Up to twice the actual damages in specified cases.
Kansas3030 days after termination of the tenancy.Kan. Stat. § 58-2550One and one-half times the amount withheld plus fees in some cases.
Kentucky3030–60 days depending on the local adopting ordinance of the URLTA.KRS § 383.580Actual damages plus possible punitive damages.
Louisiana30One month from the date the premises are vacated.La. R.S. 9:3251Greater of $300 or actual damages, plus fees, if the landlord fails willfully.
Maine3030 days after termination of the tenancy.14 M.R.S. § 6033Double the amount wrongfully withheld plus fees.
Maryland4545 days after the tenancy ends.Md. Code, Real Prop. § 8-203Up to threefold damages plus attorney’s fees.
Massachusetts3030 days after termination.Mass. Gen. Laws ch. 186 § 15BTreble damages plus attorney’s fees for violations.
Michigan3030 days after termination.MCL 554.609–.613Double the amount withheld plus fees in specified cases.
Minnesota21Three weeks after termination and demand.Minn. Stat. § 504B.178Up to twice the deposit withheld, plus $500 and fees, for bad faith.
Mississippi4545 days after termination.Miss. Code § 89-8-23Actual damages; remaining deposit returned.
Missouri3030 days after termination of the tenancy.Mo. Rev. Stat. § 535.300Up to twice the deposit if the landlord wrongfully withholds.
Montana1010 days if no deductions; 30 days if deductions are claimed.Mont. Code § 70-25-202A penalty equal to the deposit plus fees may apply.
Nebraska1414 days after termination and demand.Neb. Rev. Stat. § 76-1416One month’s rent plus damages and fees in some cases.
Nevada3030 days after termination.NRS 118A.242Up to the amount of the deposit plus damages.
New Hampshire3030 days after the tenancy ends.RSA 540-A:7Actual damages plus $1,000 and fees for willful violation.
New Jersey3030 days after the tenancy ends.N.J.S.A. 46:8-21.1Double the amount wrongfully withheld plus attorney’s fees.
New Mexico3030 days after termination.N.M. Stat. § 47-8-18Actual damages plus possible punitive damages.
New York1414 days after the tenant has vacated.N.Y. Gen. Oblig. Law § 7-108The deposit plus actual damages; willful violation can add fees.
North Carolina3030 days after the tenancy ends.N.C. Gen. Stat. § 42-52The deposit plus possible treble damages under unfair-practice law.
North Dakota3030 days after termination.N.D. Cent. Code § 47-16-07.1Treble damages for bad-faith withholding.
Ohio3030 days after termination.Ohio Rev. Code § 5321.16The amount due plus damages and attorney’s fees.
Oklahoma4530 days in some leases; 45 days is the common statutory outer window.Okla. Stat. tit. 41 § 115Double the amount wrongfully withheld.
Oregon3131 days after termination.ORS 90.300Twice the amount wrongfully withheld plus fees.
Pennsylvania3030 days after termination or upon surrender of possession.68 P.S. § 250.512Double the amount wrongfully withheld.
Rhode Island2020 days after termination.R.I. Gen. Laws § 34-18-19Twice the deposit plus fees in specified cases.
South Carolina3030 days after termination.S.C. Code § 27-40-410Three times the amount wrongfully withheld plus fees.
South Dakota14Two weeks after termination, unless the lease allows up to 45 days.S.D. Codified Laws § 43-32-24Double the deposit plus costs.
Tennessee3030 days, or 60 days if specified in a written agreement.Tenn. Code § 66-28-301The amount due plus possible additional damages.
Texas3030 days after the tenant surrenders the premises.Tex. Prop. Code §§ 92.103, 92.109Three times the portion wrongfully withheld plus $100 and attorney’s fees.
Utah3030 days after termination.Utah Code § 57-17-3$100 plus actual damages, and fees if the tenant gave a forwarding address.
Vermont1414 days after the premises are vacated.9 V.S.A. § 4461Double the amount withheld plus attorney’s fees.
Virginia4545 days after termination.Va. Code § 55.1-1226Actual damages plus attorney’s fees.
Washington2121 days after termination.RCW 59.18.280The amount withheld plus up to twice that amount as a penalty, and fees, for intentional refusal.
West Virginia6060 days after the tenancy ends.W. Va. Code § 37-6A-4The amount due plus possible costs.
Wisconsin2121 days after the tenant surrenders the premises.Wis. Stat. § 704.28Double the amount wrongfully withheld plus costs.
Wyoming3030 days after termination, or 15 days after receiving a forwarding address if later.Wyo. Stat. § 1-21-1208The deposit plus additional damages of up to $200.
