deposit

Security deposit deadlines, by state

The clock starts when you leave. Most people start it when they get angry.

Most states give the landlord between 14 and 60 days after you surrender the unit. California is 21. New York is 14. Texas is 30. Florida is 15 days if they claim nothing, 30 if they itemize.

The clock does not start when you ‘follow up.’ It starts when you leave and, in some states, when you demand in writing and give a forwarding address. Put the forwarding address in the letter. Put it in an email. Keep both.

Bad-faith penalties are the reason a dry letter works. Texas can treble the withheld portion plus $100 and fees. New Jersey doubles. Washington can add a penalty of twice the withheld amount. Cite the statute. Do not invent damages.

Use the deadlines table on this site, then confirm the current code. Legislatures edit these sections more often than blogs do.
