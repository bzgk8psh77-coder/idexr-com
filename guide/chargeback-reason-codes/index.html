Chargeback reason codes, without the bank’s fog

Match the evidence to the code or the reviewer stops reading.

A representment letter that argues ‘the customer is lying’ loses to a letter that argues Visa 13.1 with a tracking number that matches the order.

Fraud codes want AVS, CVV, 3-D Secure, device, and IP. Not-received codes want carrier scans and a signature. Not-as-described codes want the listing as the cardholder saw it, plus a refund policy shown at checkout.

Label exhibits A, B, C. Ten labeled pages beat forty screenshots named IMG_8841. Submit inside the acquirer’s window — often days, not weeks.

Cardholders: file in the issuer’s app, attach the letter, get a reference number. If the charge is criminal fraud, freeze the card first.
