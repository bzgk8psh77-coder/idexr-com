How to send a demand letter so a court will respect it

Certified mail, what to keep, and why email alone is a weak exhibit.

A demand letter is not theatre. It is the first exhibit in a file a judge might actually read. Date it. Sign it. Keep a copy that matches what left your hand.

Print two copies. Sign in ink. Mail one USPS Certified Mail, Return Receipt Requested. Stick the tracking number on the copy you keep. Email a PDF the same day so there is a second clock. Email without certified mail is easy to deny.

Do not threaten criminal charges, doxx, or reviews. Ask for a sum certain by a date certain. Cite the statute. Attach the lease and the proof of payment if you are filing soon.

If they pay, cash the check and write “payment in full of deposit” in your own notes. If they do not, the letter and the green card are how small-claims court knows you tried.
