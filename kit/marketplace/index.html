Amazon Seller / Account Review

Account: [account id]
Case / appeal ID: [case id]
Date of freeze or notice: ________

Re: Appeal — restoration of account and release of funds

I am writing a single, complete appeal.

Facts, in order:
1. I operate the account [account] on Amazon.
2. On ________ the account or payouts were restricted.
3. What happened, as I understand it: [describe the notice in their words, then yours].
4. I have not restated every ticket. The exhibits are the record.

What I am asking:
Please restore selling privileges and release eligible funds. If a specific policy still applies, tell me the exact policy ID and the cure.

Exhibit index:
A. The original notice, full text
B. Identity / KYC documents already on file (redact what they do not need)
C. Order and tracking records for any cited transactions
D. Invoices from suppliers
E. Bank or tax identity consistent with the account
F. A one-page timeline

I will not open duplicate appeals. This is the file.

[Your name]

Checklist

  • Quote their notice back to them. Do not guess the policy.
  • One issue per appeal. ‘Also my other listing’ gets the file closed.
  • Tracking that matches the order they cited
  • No templates copied from Facebook groups — reviewers flag them
  • If funds are held, ask for the exact hold code and release date

Deadlines

  • See the notice

    Appeal window · Amazon POA and Stripe/PayPal holds often expire. File inside the window on the notice, not ‘soon.’
