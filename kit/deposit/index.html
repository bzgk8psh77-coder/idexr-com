September 30, 2026

Re: Demand for return of security deposit — [rental address]

Dear [Landlord]:

I vacated the premises at [rental address] on ________. I paid a security deposit of $0.00 on or about ________. To date, no portion of the deposit has been returned. The remaining balance is $0.00.

Under Cal. Civ. Code § 1950.5, a landlord in California generally must return the deposit, or provide a written itemization of lawful deductions, within 21 days. 21 days after the tenant vacates. The statutory window in this matter ran on or about ________.

I demand payment of $0.00 to me at the forwarding address below within fourteen (14) days of this letter, by October 14, 2026. Please take notice: Up to twice the deposit in addition to actual damages for bad-faith retention.

If the amount is not received by that date I intend to pursue all remedies available, including an action in small-claims court, and to seek any statutory damages, costs, and fees the law allows. This letter is written to create a clear record. It is not a waiver of any right.

Forwarding address:
[Forwarding address]

Please govern yourselves accordingly.

Sincerely,

[Your name]

Checklist

  • Copy of the lease and any move-in checklist
  • Proof the deposit was paid (receipt, cancelled check, ledger)
  • Move-out date proof (keys returned, walkthrough, emails)
  • Photos or video of the unit on move-out day, with timestamps if you have them
  • Any itemized deduction list the landlord sent — mark what you dispute
  • Forwarding address in writing (email + this letter)
  • Two printed copies of this letter
  • USPS Certified Mail, Return Receipt Requested — keep the green card or tracking
